About

AI Accident Detection System

Real-time road accident detection using YOLOv8 and Roboflow. Instant alerts via Telegram, Email, SMS, and WhatsApp.

Technologies: Flask, YOLOv8, Roboflow, OpenCV, SQLite, Chart.js, Leaflet, ReportLab, Twilio
Team: RDMA Team

What's Inside

Dashboard

Live progress, vehicle counts, confidence & event timeline.

Detection

Upload, YouTube, RTSP/IP camera & webcam sources.

History

Searchable accident log with operator confirm / false-alarm / emergency actions.

Gallery

Captured accident frames filtered by vehicle type.

Analytics

Daily, hourly, type, severity & confidence charts with PDF export.

Map

Geolocate incidents and find nearby hospitals via Overpass.

Queue

Background video processing queue with priorities.

Vehicle Intel

License-plate capture (OpenCV) with owner lookup & registry.

Alerts

Telegram, Email (SMTP), SMS & WhatsApp (Twilio) notifications.

Settings

Thresholds, camera location, alert channels & credentials.

Note: Automatic plate text recognition requires an OCR engine (pytesseract or easyocr). Without it, the Vehicle Intel module localizes plates and supports manual confirmation via the capture log.
